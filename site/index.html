04 / Model and assurance

Every panel comes with a reason to trust it.

A checked model with a defined boundary: qualification coverage under stated supplier failures.

  1. 01

    Preserve the source

    A frozen workbook, exact product tokens and hashed mappings make each qualification traceable.

  2. 02

    Find the panels

    MILP finds a minimum-cardinality panel. Hypergraph methods enumerate inclusion-minimal alternatives.

  3. 03

    Check independently

    Matrix witnesses check feasibility and minimality. A separate MILP searches for missing panels.

What this establishes

Every product retains a qualified supplier under the modelled failures. “Minimal” means no supplier can be removed; “minimum” means least cardinality.

What still requires evidence

Capacity, cost, willingness to bid, delivery times, shared plants and common ownership. This is a research case study, not a purchasing recommendation.
