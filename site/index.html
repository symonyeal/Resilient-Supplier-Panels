04 / Method

Every panel is verified independently of the solver that found it.

  1. 01

    Freeze

    Hashed workbook and product mappings.

  2. 02

    Solve

    Exact branch and bound for the minimum; Berge, FK-B and MMCS for the alternatives.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Matrix witnesses prove minimality; a separate exhaustive search checks completeness.

Shown

Each product keeps a qualified supplier under the modelled failures. Minimal: no supplier removable. Minimum: fewest suppliers.

Not shown

Capacity, cost, willingness to bid, delivery, shared plants, common ownership. Not a purchasing recommendation.
