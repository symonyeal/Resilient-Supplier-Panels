# Qualification changes, gaps and larger baskets

The three frozen workbooks contain 780, 941 and 981 supplier identities. They were
captured on 2025-06-20, 2025-11-22 and retrieved on 2026-10-15 respectively. Capture
dates are observation dates, not effective dates of approvals. The original 88-case
campaign and its timings are unchanged.

## Backup approvals changed in both directions

| Observation | Precise products | Products with one supplier | October campaign baskets feasible at k = 1 | Baskets containing absent products |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| June 2025 | 469 | 221 | 8 of 40 | 4 baskets |
| November 2025 | 471 | 220 | 10 of 40 | 2 baskets |
| October 2026 | 451 | 217 | 10 of 40 | 0 baskets |

Among products present at both endpoints, three gained a second supplier and one lost
one between June and November. Between November and October, none gained a second
supplier and three lost one. The falling number of single-source products alone does
not establish improvement: products also enter and leave the list. Exact product names
and supplier-key additions/removals are in
[`change-seed1-v0.4.json`](../../results/change-seed1-v0.4.json).

Suppliers are compared by cleaned name, billing address and ZIP5; products by the strict
catalog. Renames or address changes can appear as exits and entries. Older spelling
errors are retained unless covered by an existing mapping. These are observed list
changes, not verified business openings, closures or approval decisions.

## Which second approval helps the sampled baskets most?

Seeds 1 through 1000 produce 40,000 primary basket draws, of which 11,256 are feasible
at k = 1 (28.14%). Baskets can repeat across seeds. This population mixes equal numbers
of uniform and overlap draws within each class and size; it does not represent demand.

| Product | Baskets it blocks | Baskets it alone blocks |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| PAN1 - pancakes whole wheat frozen | 3,032 | 402 |
| MX1 - exchange of NDM for processed commodities | 1,615 | 210 |
| 311211 CSSM corn-soy/instant corn-soy | 2,482 | 173 |
| 311212 KCPG milled rice (bulk) | 2,195 | 172 |
| 100138 - beef chili without beans, canned | 4,065 | 170 |

An additional independent approval for PAN1 alone would make 402 currently infeasible
draws feasible, holding all other qualifications fixed. This assumes the approval is
attainable; it specifies neither the new supplier nor its capacity. Uniform draws make
single-source products within a class exchangeable, so the ranking reflects overlap
draws and sampling variation. The saved output includes exact combinatorial uniform
shares by class and basket size, plus a greedy sequence of approvals. The sequence is
a heuristic, without an optimality claim.

## Identity evidence and joint failures

Every one of the 31 candidate pairs now has an evidence entry: six have matching-address
evidence supporting a duplicate-row sensitivity, 13 have evidence supporting distinct
billing rows of a common entity, and 12 remain unresolved. The duplicate-row sensitivity
changes no seed-1 primary feasibility outcome; merging all candidates still changes two.
The strict matrix remains the original row-identity interpretation. A common UEI alone
does not establish a common physical site.

The owner catalog contains 44 member entries in 15 groups. Entity scenarios also join
the supported identity pairs transitively. These are explicit joint-outage assumptions
informed by recipient-parent records, company filings and named-facility sources. They
are not estimated failure probabilities, an exhaustive ownership map, or verified
shared-plant relationships. The review log below distinguishes direct records from
inferences. In 40,000 draws, 637 of the 11,256 baskets feasible at k = 1 fail the Goodman
Food Products entity scenario, leaving 10,619 feasible under all modeled scenarios.

[`entities-seeds1000-v0.4.json`](../../results/entities-seeds1000-v0.4.json) contains the
members, exposed products and per-group break counts. The scenarios preserve supplier
rows; only the separately reported duplicate-row sensitivity merges them.

## Award totals cannot supply a price or capacity objective

The retained API collection contains 13,798 distinct AMS contract awards selected through
three activity windows, FY2024 through FY2026. A name-core and ZIP rule proposes UEI
matches for 341 of 981 supplier identities (330 distinct UEIs), covering $13,011,216,165.06
of the recipients' full `Award Amount` values in the retrieved records. Amounts are
deduplicated by award identifier before aggregation and by UEI within each group.

These are candidate matches. A billing ZIP or performance ZIP is corroboration, not a
supplier identifier. Full award amounts are not fiscal-window transaction spending,
plant revenue, unit prices, capacity, or QBL-product expenditure. Overlapping entity
groups' dollar totals must not be added. No monetary optimization is justified by
these records. Comparable quantities, units, delivery periods and a verified
supplier/product join remain necessary. Compressed evidence and hashes are retained in
[`data/evidence`](../../data/evidence); `python analysis/check_evidence.py` reconciles
every published recipient amount to the distinct raw awards.

## Whole-class baskets exceed listing scale

Every full class is infeasible at k = 1 because it includes single-source products.
Restricting to products with two or more suppliers gives:

| Class | Products | Eligible suppliers | Minimum panel | Minimal panels |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| description | 169 | 340 | 101 | 75,180,897,792 |
| programme | 30 | 179 | 31 | not established: count timed out at 10 seconds |
| NAICS | 23 | 94 | 15 | 21,924 |
| material | 12 | 22 | 13 | 510 |

The three complete counts use MODEL Theorem 4; they do not list or independently exhaust
the expanded families. Every reported minimum has a matrix-feasible, minimal panel;
optimality comes from the exact branch and bound. The description count has 1,568
signature orbits, illustrating how counting can succeed beyond feasible listing size.
These observations apply to one workbook, one machine and the predeclared limits.
A separately retained 60-second follow-up on the programme-class instance also timed out
([result](../../results/program-min2-count60-v0.4.json)); it does not replace the protocol run.
